How Khoj Works

A straightforward 3-step process connecting families, hospitals, and police.

Step 1
Family Files a Report

Register and submit a missing person report with physical description, Aadhaar number, photos, district, and contact details. Matching runs automatically on submission.

Step 2
Hospital Uploads a Patient

Hospital staff records an unidentified patient — estimated age, physical features, clothing, any ID found, and etc. Matching runs automatically on upload.

Step 3
System Finds Matches

The matching engine scores compatibility across several factors — gender, blood group, age, district, marks, clothing, and photo similarity. Both parties are notified instantly.

The Engine

Rule-based. Transparent.

Who Uses Khoj

Two distinct roles, each with their own secure dashboard and login flow.

Families

File up to 3 active missing person reports. Add search updates and clues. Get notified when a potential hospital match is found. Hospital contact revealed only on a valid match.

Hospital Staff

Upload unidentified patient records with 3 photos. Receive alerts when a family match is found. View family contact and mark patients as identified.

Important: Khoj is a coordination and support tool only. It does not replace official police investigation systems, FIR registration, or any legal process. Always file a proper FIR with your local police station when reporting a missing person.
