Platform Overview

About Khoj

A rule-based coordination platform connecting families and hospitals, to help resolve missing person cases.

The Problem

Thousands of missing person cases in India remain unresolved due to poor coordination between families, hospitals, and intake facilities.

Why Khoj was built

When a person goes missing, families often search blindly across jurisdictions. Meanwhile, an unidentified or trauma patient matching that description may already be admitted to a hospital nearby — but there is no unified system connecting these two critical pieces of information.

Khoj bridges this gap by maintaining an active, matched database of missing person reports and unidentified patient records — with transparent scoring to surface likely matches directly to both parties.

What Khoj Does

Missing Person Reports
Families file detailed reports with physical description, government ID (optional), clothing, photos, relation to the missing person, and last known location. Max 3 active cases per family user.
Unidentified Patient Records
Hospital staff upload patient records with 3 intake photos (face, full body, side profile), clothing description, and any ID found on the person — last 4 digits only, for privacy.
Automated Matching Engine
Matching Engine is triggered on every save. Pairs first pass strict demographic and timeline filters, followed by scoring across 11 weighted factors. Matches above 40% confidence are stored with full breakdowns.
Direct Match Alerts
Both hospital staff and family members receive instant in-app alerts when a high-confidence match is discovered, allowing direct phone or email verification.

How the Matching Engine Works

Khoj uses a hybrid scoring system combining computer vision face biometrics with weighted demographic attributes. Every factor is transparent and explainable. The per-field score is stored with every match so you can see exactly why it was flagged.

Pre-Match Verification: Strict Gender match (Male↔Male, Female↔Female, Other↔Other) and Timeline Validity (patient admitted on or after last seen date) act as mandatory hard filters before biometric and demographic scoring runs.

What Khoj Does NOT Do

It is not an official police or government platform — it is a direct family-to-hospital coordination bridge.
It does not replace official First Information Report (FIR) filing or legal missing person procedures.
It does not verify institutional or hospital IDs through external government databases.
There is no police role on this platform — all workflows operate strictly between families and medical facilities.
It does not guarantee positive legal identification — matches are probabilistic leads that require human verification.
Important Disclaimer

Always file a proper FIR with your local police station when reporting a missing person. Khoj is a supplementary coordination tool and does not constitute an official legal report. The platform makes no guarantees about match accuracy, and all matches must be verified through direct contact between the reporting family and hospital administration.
